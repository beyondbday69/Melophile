## Features ✨️    
- Play music from YouTube Music or YouTube for free, without ads and in the background
- Three Now Playing styles: Classic, Material 3 Expressive and Apple Music
- Ten-band equalizer with presets and AutoEq headphone profiles, plus Delay and Reverb effects
- Melophile Wrapped: your year in music, plus monthly recap playlists
- On-device listening analytics: charts, period history, listening clock
- Word-by-word Apple Music-style lyrics, romanization for 12 languages, share lyrics as an image
- Home screen widgets: turntable, playlists and listening insights
- High quality up-to 256kbps stream (Opus or AAC) for YouTube Music Premium users
- Browsing Home, Charts, Podcast, Moods & Genre with YouTube Music data at high speed    
- Search everything on YouTube    
- Spotify Canvas and Animated Album Art supported
- Power your experience with Melophile Charts
- Play 1080p video option with subtitle    
- AI song suggestions    
- Import playlists converted from Spotify and other apps
- Customize your playlist, synced with YouTube Music
- Notifications from followed artists    
- Caching and offline playback support    
- Crossfade with DJ-style like Apple Music
- Customizing THEME (Light, Dark, Color, Material 3 Dynamic Artwork palette, etc.)
- Synced lyrics from Melophile Lyrics, LRCLIB, Spotify (require login) and YouTube Transcript - AI lyrics translation (BETA) (\*)  
- Personalize data (\**) and multi-YouTube-account support    
- Last.fm scrobbling (Full version)
- Supports SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike
- Sleep Timer    
- Android Auto with online content, feature rich UI/UX
- Discord Rich Presence support
- Listen Together: shared rooms that play in sync with friends, compatible with Metrolist
- And many more!    
  
> (\*) Use your OpenAI or Gemini API key    
> (\**) For users who chose "Send back to Google" feature    
    
> **Notice**    
> This app is actively developed. If you encounter any bugs, please create an issue on GitHub or reach out on the Discord server.   
> Because it interacts with YouTube Music endpoints, occasional playback upstream quirks may happen.

## Data & Services    
- Hidden API & stream extraction for YouTube Music
- Spotify Web API integration for Spotify Canvas & Lyrics
- Inspired originally by [InnerTune](https://github.com/z-huang/InnerTune/)
- Stream extraction powered by [SmartTube](https://github.com/yuliskov/SmartTube)
- [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app/) integration to skip sponsored segments
- ReturnYouTubeDislike for ratings & feedback
- Multi-provider lyrics from Melophile Lyrics and [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net/)

## Privacy    
Melophile does not have any trackers or third-party servers collecting your personal data in the FOSS version. If YouTube logged-in users enable the optional "Send back to Google" feature, Melophile only uses the YouTube Music Tracking API to sync history to Google for recommendation personalization.

## Desktop App

### Which file should I download?
- **Windows**: Download the `.msix` package and run `install.bat` to install.
- **macOS**: Download the `.dmg` installer.
- **Linux**: Download the `.AppImage` (runs on all modern Linux distributions).

## FAQ    
#### 1. Wrong Lyrics?    
Lyrics are provided by LRCLIB and other sources. Sometimes lyrics may not match perfectly with the YouTube `videoId` parameter. In those cases, string and duration matchers are used. You can vote or submit corrections directly within the lyrics view.

#### 2. What does "Melophile" mean?    
A *melophile* is someone who has an abnormal love of or passion for music. Melophile is engineered for music lovers who appreciate clean aesthetics, rich audio controls, and an open, distraction-free listening experience.

## Legal Disclaimer & Terms of Use

### 1. 100% Free, Open-Source & Strictly Non-Commercial
Melophile is a fully open-source project (FOSS) created for educational purposes and personal use. **We do not sell this application, nor do we monetize it in any way.** There are no advertisements, no premium features, no subscriptions, and no hidden fees within the app.

### 2. A Custom Browser with Content Filtering
Melophile acts strictly as a specialized, third-party web client. It parses publicly available website content and APIs of YouTube and YouTube Music, rendering them in a custom user interface.

### 3. Support Content Creators
We deeply respect the hard work of artists, musicians, and content creators. **We strongly encourage all users to subscribe to [YouTube Premium](https://www.youtube.com/premium).**

### 4. No Hosting of Copyrighted Material
We do not host, upload, distribute, or store any audio, video, or copyrighted media files on our own servers. All content accessed through this application is stored entirely on Google's/YouTube's servers.
