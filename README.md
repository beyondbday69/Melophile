> **Notice & Upstream Status (Honest Truth)**  
> Melophile is an independent open-source client and fork developed for Android and Desktop using Compose Multiplatform.
> - **Playback & APIs:** Media streaming relies on reverse-engineered YouTube Music and YouTube internal endpoints (similar to NewPipe / SmartTube / InnerTune). Whenever YouTube alters their client payloads or streaming flags, playback issues or transient stream throttling may occur until patches are applied.
> - **FOSS Non-Commercial:** This project does not sell subscriptions, does not show ads, and does not charge any fee.
> - **Support Creators:** We strongly advocate supporting music artists directly and subscribing to [YouTube Premium](https://www.youtube.com/premium) if you can.

## Features ✨️    
- **Background & Ad-Free Playback:** Stream audio in the background with screen off.
- **Player Interfaces:** Multiple Now Playing views including Material 3 Expressive, Classic, and Apple Music styles.
- **Audio Control:** 10-band equalizer, AutoEq headphone correction curves, plus reverb and delay effects.
- **Lyrics Integration:** Synchronized lyrics via LRCLIB and community lyric backends, with romanization and line-by-line sync where available.
- **Music Exploration:** Search YouTube Music catalog, explore moods & genres, new releases, and charts.
- **Home Screen Widgets:** Turntable player, quick playlists, and listening statistics widgets.
- **Local Playback & Cache:** Caching for offline replay.
- **Theming:** Material You dynamic artwork palettes, dark/light themes, and customizable accent colors.
- **Desktop Support:** Native desktop builds for Linux, Windows, and macOS via Compose Multiplatform.
- **Sleep Timer & Android Auto:** Night timer and connected car interface.

## Privacy 🔒
- **Zero Tracking:** No tracking SDKs, no telemetry harvesting, no third-party marketing trackers in standard builds.
- **Optional Google History:** Only if you explicitly log in and choose to sync history back to your YouTube account will the app contact Google recommendation endpoints.

## Desktop Installation

- **Windows**: Download `.msix` or executable from [Releases](https://github.com/beyondbday69/SimpMusic/releases).
- **macOS**: Download the `.dmg` package.
- **Linux**: Download and run the `.AppImage`.
